## Playback devices that stay visible

- Paired computers remain discoverable while idle. Background presence updates no longer interrupt Connect settings or disable controls.
- Free accounts can see their devices, connection status and current track in the player-bar picker. Playback handoff and remote controls still require Connect or Supporter.
- Compact device cards distinguish Linux, macOS, Windows and smartphones. Updated clients share their device type inside encrypted presence; older clients use recognizable names or a generic computer icon.

## Move playback and keep control

- The player bar shows where playback is happening, including the remote track, progress and volume. Playback buttons and system media keys control the selected computer.
- Transfers preserve the queue and position. The source pauses after the destination reports ready audio, and updated clients confirm that pause. An unconfirmed destination stops its transferred playback instead of continuing alongside the source.
- Temporary message failures retry safely. Offline remote controls stay disabled, and failures or timeouts leave the source playing.
- Choose **Continue here** to bring playback back, or select a third device to move the active source's queue there.

## Listen to different things on different computers

- Choose **Listen independently here** to return to the local player while the other computer keeps playing. You can then choose different music, radio or an audiobook on this computer.
- Each device keeps its own queue, volume and playback controls. Pausing locally or receiving a presence update does not silently switch an independent listener back to remote controls.
- Explicit handoff remains available when you want to move playback between the devices again.

## Cleaner and more accessible device settings

- A single play/pause control replaces separate Play and Pause buttons. Volume uses a percentage; seeking shows elapsed and total time.
- Volume and position sliders apply on release without extra confirmation buttons. Device access management lives in the device's options menu and retains its revocation confirmation.
- Controls have device-specific accessible names, visible keyboard focus and 44-point targets. Screen readers can adjust slider values directly, and Escape restores focus after closing a device menu.
- Improved layouts for light and dark themes, long device names, scrolling lists and text enlarged to 200%. English and Italian are included.

## Compatibility

- Update paired desktop clients to 0.1.5 for confirmed source-pause protection and forwarding playback to a third device. Older clients retain their existing two-device handoff behavior.
- No Connect server deployment or entitlement changes are needed for these improvements. Both players must remain open and online, with access to the relevant music or audiobook server. Connect does not relay audio.
- Device icons identify the client type; this release does not add Connect services to the iPhone app.
