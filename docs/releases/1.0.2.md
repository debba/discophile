## Podcast improvements

- Sort podcast episodes from newest or oldest on desktop, iPhone and Android. Playback follows the chosen order.
- Reimporting an existing podcast preserves its subscription and episode identifiers instead of recreating it.

## Android

- Improve responsiveness when opening Listen Now and switching library tabs.
