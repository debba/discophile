## New

- **Discophile Connect on desktop.** Use the official service or a compatible independent instance. Connect is optional: local listening, downloads and direct server connections remain free.
- **Encrypted configuration sync.** Select the music, radio and Audiobookshelf connections you want to share, plus optional language and appearance preferences. Pair trusted computers with your private recovery code; signing in alone cannot decrypt your configuration.
- **Encrypted configuration backups.** Connect and Supporter include automatic and manual snapshots: up to 64 backups retained for 30 days. Review a restored configuration before publishing it to your other computers.
- **Remote playback controls.** Control playback, navigation, seeking and volume on another online paired computer.
- **Continue here.** Transfer playback queue and position between paired computers. The source keeps playing until the destination is ready. Both computers need access to the matching library; Connect never relays audio.

## Your account, within reach

- A profile control in the top-right corner shows the picture selected on Connect, with initials as a fallback. Its menu brings together your name, email, plan, profile management, Connect settings and sign-out.
- The library selector has its own coordinated menu, with the active library clearly marked.
- **Sign in from the first launch.** The first introduction screen now offers an immediate route to Connect sign-in. You can still continue without an account.
- Account menus support keyboard navigation, light and dark themes, and larger text sizes.

## Plans and activation

- **Free:** 2 paired computers, one selected synchronized connection, and shared preferences. New connections are synchronized only after you select them in Connect settings.
- **Connect:** 10 paired computers, all selected connections, backups, remote control and playback handoff — €2.99/month or €24.99/year.
- **Supporter:** the same operational features, with a larger contribution to development — €4.99/month or €39.99/year. Optional recognition and previews remain planned benefits.
- Request activation by email to **info@discophile.app**. There is no checkout, automatic payment or renewal.

## Recovery and control

- Review paired computers, revoke access, export encrypted cloud data or delete the cloud vault.
- Revocation requires a new encryption key. Remaining trusted computers import the updated recovery code; keep it privately because the service cannot recover lost keys.
- Concurrent configuration edits require an explicit choice. Importing preserves local connections and downloads. Audiobookshelf requires a separate sign-in on each computer.
- The public Connect protocol and self-hosting guide describe the optional desktop service extensions and profile picture support.

This release covers desktop and Connect. The iPhone app is unchanged.
