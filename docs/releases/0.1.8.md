## See what plays on your other devices

- While another device plays, the player bar shows its artwork, title and artist instead of a plain title. Select it to open Now Playing for that device, with large artwork, album, timeline, play and pause, previous and next, volume, **Continue here** and **Listen independently here**.
- Artwork loads only when this computer is signed in to the same music library or Audiobookshelf account, using its own credentials. Nothing is fetched from the other device.
- Shared presence now carries the playing item's type, title, artist, album and artwork reference, encrypted like the rest of the presence. Devices that do not send these details still show their title.

## Compatibility

- No Connect server deployment or entitlement changes are needed.
- Artwork and details appear for phones running Discophile for Android 0.6.0 or later and the matching iOS build. Older apps keep working and show the plain title.
