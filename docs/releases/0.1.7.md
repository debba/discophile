## Pick up where you stopped

- Podcast episodes and tracks of at least 20 minutes remember your listening position and synchronize it with Subsonic, Navidrome or Jellyfin. Track lists show time remaining or Played, with actions to mark an item as played or unplayed.
- Playlists remember the track and position where you stopped on this computer, with Continue from track to resume.
- During podcast episodes, previous and next controls skip 15 seconds backward or forward across the player, media keys, tray and keyboard shortcuts. Podcast playback speed is kept across launches.
- The sleep timer offers End of episode for podcasts and pauses on the next item when the episode finishes.

## Compatibility

- No Connect server deployment or entitlement changes are needed.
- Playlist resume points are stored on each device. Episode and long-track synchronization uses your music server.
